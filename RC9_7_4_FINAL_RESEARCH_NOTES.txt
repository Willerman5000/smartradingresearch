RC9.7.4 FINAL RESEARCH FREEZE
============================

Goal: close the Validation gap without expanding the research architecture.

Final changes
- Validation no longer downloads up to 2,400 full findings in one paged join.
- It reads bounded current outputs from EXECUTION / RISK / STRATEGY / TRADERS and classifies whatever was successfully loaded.
- Validation dashboard cache is invalidated immediately after a successful classification run, so a previous 0-row snapshot does not remain visible for 15 minutes.
- Validation Free-plan egress ceiling is 4 MB/day; the other four Research services remain capped at 2 MB/day.
- On Free-plan mode, Validation no longer duplicates the four heavy search lanes through causal rescue, even if an old Render environment still says RESEARCH_CAUSAL_VALIDATION_RESCUE=1.
- The 60-cell research contract remains unchanged: execution 8 + risk 16 + strategy 12 + traders 24.
- Research VERSION is intentionally unchanged to preserve the evidence already generated by RC9.7.1 instead of resetting the new database again.
- No OOS/holdout threshold, Safety rule, leverage rule or production authority was relaxed.

Expected after deploy
- Evidence engines continue producing findings.
- Validation should begin producing OBSERVE / VALIDATION_REQUIRED / REJECTED_OOS / SHADOW_READY classifications as evidence permits.
- 0 Champions is still valid until a candidate passes every required gate.
